# Persona: Dr. Aram Petrosyan

Medical director at a contract research organization. Thoracic oncologist.

## What he says first
"A partner wants to use MKI67 expression from RNA-seq as a proliferation score. The idea: patients with stage I lung adenocarcinoma and high MKI67 get adjuvant chemotherapy in a pilot study. Before I agree, I need to know if high MKI67 predicts worse overall survival in stage I, and if it adds anything beyond age and sex. Use TCGA lung adenocarcinoma."

## Answers he gives when asked
- Decision: support the pilot design with MKI67 as the selection marker, or ask the partner for a better marker. He decides with the partner's scientific board.
- Reader: himself and the board. Clinicians.
- Cost of error: chemotherapy for patients who do not need it (toxicity). Missing patients who need it is also bad, but the pilot is small.
- Population: stage I primary tumors (IA, IB, and unspecified I).
- Endpoint: overall survival. He does not know how TCGA records it.
- Deadline: one week, one page.
