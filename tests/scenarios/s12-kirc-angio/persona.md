# Persona: Dr. Lena Okafor

Head of translational bioinformatics at a small biotech company. The company plans a phase II trial of a new VEGFR inhibitor in clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC).

## What she says first
"Our clinical team wants to enroll only patients with a high angiogenesis score in the new trial of our VEGFR inhibitor. The score is the mean of six genes: VEGFA, KDR, ESM1, PECAM1, ANGPTL4, CD34. They say the score finds the patients who benefit from anti-angiogenic drugs, and they want proof from TCGA kidney clear cell data before the protocol meeting. Use the TCGA KIRC RNA-seq and clinical data."

## Answers she gives when asked
- Decision: use the score as an enrollment criterion in the trial protocol, use it only as a stratification factor, or do not use it. She recommends; the chief medical officer decides.
- Reader: the chief medical officer (an oncologist) and the trial statistician.
- Cost of error: a wrong enrollment criterion excludes patients who could benefit and can make the trial fail. A missed useful criterion costs a less efficient trial.
- High score: the clinical team said "above the median". She does not know if this is right.
- Patients in TCGA: she does not know which treatments they got.
- Endpoint: the clinical team said "survival". She does not know which survival.
- Deadline: one week. Format: one page and a few slides.

She does not know anything about the data files.
