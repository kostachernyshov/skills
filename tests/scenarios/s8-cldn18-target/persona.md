# Persona: Dr. Kenji Watanabe

Vice president of business development at a biotech company that makes antibody-drug conjugates (ADC).

## What he says first
"A partner offers us a license for an ADC against Claudin 18.2. Before the term sheet I need two answers. First: in which tumors is the target expressed, and in what fraction of patients: gastric, pancreatic, others? Second: is it safe, that is, is the target absent from normal tissues? Use public data: TCGA tumors and GTEx normal tissues, as gene and transcript tables."

## Answers he gives when asked
- Decision: sign the term sheet for gastric only, for gastric and pancreatic, or walk away. He decides with the CEO and the head of R&D.
- Reader: the CEO (no biology) and the head of R&D (antibody chemist).
- Cost of error: a license for a target that hits normal tissue is a costly clinical failure. Walking away from a good target loses a market.
- Success: he wants a fraction of eligible patients per tumor type, and a list of normal tissues with expression. He does not know which expression level makes a patient "eligible"; he asks for a recommendation.
- He thinks "18.2" is just the name of the target. He does not know about isoforms.
- Deadline: ten days. Format: one page and a few slides.

He does not know anything about the data files.
