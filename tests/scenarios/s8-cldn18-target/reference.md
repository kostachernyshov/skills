# Reference answer (grader only)

ENST00000343735 is the stomach isoform (CLDN18.2): GTEx stomach median about 199 TPM, other GTEx tissues about 0, GTEx lung median 3.5 (20% of lungs > 10 TPM). ENST00000183605 is the lung isoform (CLDN18.1): GTEx lung median about 70 TPM, TCGA adjacent lung about 390. Gene-level CLDN18 is high in normal lung because of 18.1.
Primary tumors with 18.2 > 10 TPM: STAD 78%, PAAD 74%, ESCA 35% (adenocarcinoma part), LUAD 6%; > 100 TPM: 47%, 35%, 25%, 3%.
Safety: 18.2 is in normal stomach (on-target, off-tumor; nausea and vomiting with zolbetuximab). Eligibility in trials is IHC (>= 75% of tumor cells 2+/3+): about 38% of gastric cancers (SPOTLIGHT/GLOW screening); mRNA overestimates and cannot set eligibility.
