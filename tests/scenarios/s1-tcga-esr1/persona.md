# Persona: Dr. Lena Ortiz

Head of translational research at a small oncology diagnostics company. Not a bioinformatician.

## What she says first
"We plan a trial with an ER-positive arm. Central IHC for ER is slow and expensive. Can we use ESR1 mRNA from RNA-seq instead to assign patients? I need a yes or no, and a cutoff if yes. Use TCGA breast data."

## Answers she gives when asked
- Decision: use RNA-based ER call for trial screening, or keep central IHC. She decides, with the trial steering committee.
- Reader: herself and the committee. They know oncology, not statistics.
- Cost of error: an ER-negative patient in the ER-positive arm gets endocrine therapy that does not help. This is worse than the opposite error.
- Acceptable disagreement with IHC: she does not know. She asks for a recommendation.
- Population: primary breast tumors of the kind that enter trials.
- Deadline: one week. Format: one page.
- Prior work: she heard that mRNA and IHC agree well, but has no reference.

She does not know anything about the data files.
