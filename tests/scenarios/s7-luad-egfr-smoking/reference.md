# Reference answer (grader only)

Heavy smokers (>= 30 pack-years, current or reformed, with MC3 calls): 11 of 233 (4.7%, Wilson 95% 2.7-8.3%) have a sensitizing EGFR variant (exon 19 deletion, L858R, L861Q, G719X, S768I). Answer: no, do not skip the test. Published: EGFR in current/former smokers about 5-15% (western series); guidelines (CAP/IASLC/AMP 2018, NCCN) test all non-squamous NSCLC regardless of smoking.
