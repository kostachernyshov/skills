# Persona: Dr. Tomasz Nowak

Director of a molecular diagnostics lab. Runs RNA-seq panels on colorectal tumors.

## What he says first
"We already sequence RNA from every colorectal tumor. Can we flag MSI-high tumors from the expression data, so that we can drop the separate MSI PCR or mismatch-repair IHC test? Use TCGA colorectal data."

## Answers he gives when asked
- Decision: replace the MSI test with an RNA call, use the RNA call as a pre-screen, or keep the test. He decides with the lab quality manager.
- Reader: the lab quality manager and two pathologists.
- Cost of error: a missed MSI-high tumor means a patient may miss immunotherapy and Lynch syndrome screening. A false MSI-high call costs one confirmatory test.
- Population: primary colon and rectal cancers.
- Acceptable performance: he does not know. He asks for a recommendation.
- Deadline: one week. Format: one page and a few slides for the quality meeting.
