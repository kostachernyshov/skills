# Persona: Dr. Ines Moreau

Head of bioinformatics at a diagnostics company. The company sells a 500-gene tumor panel that reports tumor mutational burden (TMB).

## What she says first
"Our product team analysed TCGA and says: patients with TMB-high tumors live longer, in all cancers. They want to print 'TMB-high: favorable prognosis' in every patient report, and the CEO wants to publish it as a new finding. I own the bioinformatics behind the report, so I must check it first. Use TCGA mutation calls and the TCGA survival table."

## Answers she gives when asked
- Decision: put the prognostic sentence in the report for all patients, put it only for some cancer types, or not at all; and publish or not. She recommends; the CEO and the medical director decide.
- Reader: the CEO (business) and the medical director (oncologist).
- Cost of error: a wrong prognostic sentence in a medical report is a regulatory and liability risk. Not printing a true sentence costs little.
- TMB-high: the product team used 10 mutations per megabase, as the panel does. She does not know how to compute TMB from exome calls; she asks for a recommendation.
- Patients in TCGA: she does not know which treatments they got.
- Endpoint: the product team used overall survival. She asks if this is right.
- Deadline: two weeks. Format: one page and a few slides.

She does not know anything about the data files.
