# Persona: Dr. Lena Vogt

Bioinformatician in a small diagnostics start-up (8 people). The start-up sells RNA tests for breast cancer to hospital labs.

## What she says first
"We downloaded TCGA breast cancer data. Can you find something useful in it about ESR1? I think it can help us somehow."

## Answers she gives when asked
- Who gets the benefit: she does not know at first. If the agent asks who gets the benefit and gives no options, say: "I am not sure. I just want something useful from these data."
- If the agent gives options for who gets the benefit, choose the option that is closest to: "the lab teams of our customer hospitals, who must decide if an RNA ESR1 result can back up an unclear IHC ER result". If no option is close, write this sentence as your own option.
- Decision: the start-up decides if it adds an "ESR1 RNA" line to its test report. The CEO decides; she recommends.
- Reader: the CEO and the medical advisor (a pathologist).
- Cost of error: a wrong ER call can give a patient the wrong hormone therapy. Not adding the line costs little.
- Deadline: two days. She also wants "a validation on an independent cohort" in these two days. She has no second cohort.
- Success threshold: she does not know.
- If the agent says that two things she wants do not fit together, accept the recommendation of the agent.
- If the agent proposes a method or a plausibility statement and it fits, say "yes".

She does not know anything about the data files.
