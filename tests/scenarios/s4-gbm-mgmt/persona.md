# Persona: Dr. Sofia Lindqvist

Head of clinical operations at a neuro-oncology contract research organization.

## What she says first
"A sponsor runs a trial of a new drug added to temozolomide in newly diagnosed glioblastoma. They want to enrol mainly patients with a methylated MGMT promoter. Our partner lab can run methylation arrays. Can we use array data to call MGMT status, and do patients with a methylated MGMT promoter really live longer on temozolomide? Use TCGA glioblastoma data."

## Answers she gives when asked
- Decision: use an array-based MGMT call for enrichment, or require the standard MGMT test (pyrosequencing or methylation-specific PCR) at a central lab. She decides with the sponsor.
- Reader: the sponsor's clinical team. Oncologists.
- Cost of error: a patient with an unmethylated promoter called methylated enters a trial where the benefit is unlikely. Missing methylated patients slows enrolment only.
- Population: adults with newly diagnosed primary glioblastoma.
- Endpoint: overall survival.
- She does not know if TCGA has a standard MGMT test result.
- Deadline: two weeks. Format: one page and a few slides for the sponsor meeting.
