# Persona: Dr. Hannah Okafor

Chief scientific officer of a biotech that sells an immune-profiling RNA assay.

## What she says first
"Our marketing team wants to tell oncologists that a high T-cell / cytotoxic gene score predicts longer survival in melanoma. Is this claim true? Use TCGA melanoma data. I need a clear yes or no before we print the brochure."

## Answers she gives when asked
- Decision: put the prognostic claim in the brochure, put a weaker claim, or no claim. She decides with the head of marketing and the regulatory lead.
- Reader: the head of marketing (no statistics) and the regulatory lead.
- Cost of error: a false claim is a regulatory and reputation risk. Too weak a claim loses some sales.
- Population: patients with cutaneous melanoma, any stage, as their customers test.
- Score: she has no fixed gene list; she asks for a recommendation based on published work.
- Deadline: ten days. Format: one page and a few slides.
