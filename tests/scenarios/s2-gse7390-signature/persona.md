# Persona: Marc Weber

Vice president of business development at a diagnostics start-up. Background in biology, MBA.

## What he says first
"A company offers us a license for the 76-gene prognostic signature for node-negative breast cancer (Wang et al., 2005). Before we pay, tell me: does it predict distant metastasis in an independent cohort, and does it add anything beyond grade, size, age and ER? We have GEO GSE7390 as tables."

## Answers he gives when asked
- Decision: negotiate the license (and the price), or walk away. He decides with the CEO.
- Reader: the CEO and himself. No statistics background beyond hazard ratio.
- Cost of error: paying for a signature that adds nothing is a 2-year loss. Walking away from a good signature can be fixed later at a higher price.
- Success: a clear statement "adds / does not add / cannot tell from these data", with the reason.
- Deadline: 5 days.
- The license company will give the exact signature weights only after signing.

He does not know anything about the data files.
